Webcam eye-tracking · reading stress

Track reading stress
from gaze and blinks.

A browser-based non-invasive study tool. Load a text, set the timer, calibrate gaze, and collect oculomotor metrics: fixations, saccades, and blinks to estimate cognitive load while reading.

  • 468
    MediaPipe Face Landmarker with iris tracking for gaze detection, using the same model as the desktop pipeline.
  • 45
    Multi-click calibration (9 points x 5 clicks) with regression and head-motion compensation.
  • 4
    Stress score built from four signals: blink rate, dispersion, scan speed, and downward gaze.

Before you start

  • Use Google Chrome and allow webcam access.
  • Sit about ~60 cm from the screen, in a well-lit room, with light in front of you (not behind).
  • Keep your head relatively still during calibration and testing.
  • Avoid reflective glasses or strong backlighting.
Note. A consumer webcam does not match a lab-grade infrared eye tracker: word-level precision is limited. Stress metrics based on relative gaze and blinks are still useful.
